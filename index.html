05 / Why it works

Active thinking.
Gentle by nature.

Effective doesn’t have to mean aggressive. These are the three ingredients doing the work, and what each one is actually for.

01

PAP & V34

Two peroxide-free routes to a brighter smile — PAP works gradually over a week of strips, V34 pigments correct colour the moment you brush.

02

Nano-hydroxyapatite

A mineral close to what enamel is already made of, which is why it is comfortable for people who find whitening toothpastes harsh.

03

Xylitol

A naturally derived sweetener used throughout the toothpaste range, so daily brushing tastes mild rather than medicinal.
